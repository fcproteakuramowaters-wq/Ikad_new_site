# Ikad Hotels: Plan to Rank #1 in Victoria Island

The website is now technically strong. Fast pages, correct structured data, location content and clear booking paths are all in place. But **for "hotel in Victoria Island" searches, Google Maps and TripAdvisor rankings are mostly decided off the website.** They are decided by reviews, your Google Business Profile and how consistently the hotel is listed across the web.

This plan lists what to do, in priority order. Everything here is allowed by Google's and TripAdvisor's rules; shortcuts that break them (see the end) can get the hotel delisted.

---

## 1. Google Business Profile (weeks 1–2), the biggest single lever

Most "hotel near me" and "hotels in Victoria Island" searches show the **Google Maps "local pack"** before any website. That ranking comes from your Business Profile.

For **each** hotel (Victoria Island and Yaba), at [business.google.com](https://business.google.com):

1. **Claim and verify** the profile. If a "Coolio Hotel" profile exists for Borno Way, claim it and rename it to **Ikad Hotel Yaba**. Don't create a duplicate.
2. **Name:** use the real signage name only (`Ikad Hotel & Suites` / `Ikad Hotel Yaba`). Adding keywords like "Best Hotel Victoria Island" breaks Google's rules and gets profiles suspended.
3. **Address, phone and website** must match the website footer *exactly*:
   - VI: `204B Etim Inyang Crescent, Eko Hotel Roundabout, Victoria Island, Lagos` · `+234 916 373 8458` · `https://ikadhotels.com/victoria-island`
   - Yaba: `270 Borno Way, Adekunle, Yaba, Lagos` · `+234 814 731 8331` · `https://ikadhotels.com/yaba`
4. **Category:** primary **Hotel**. Add **Restaurant** and **Bar** only if they are open to non-guests.
5. **Hotel attributes:** fill in every one that is true: Wi-Fi, 24-hour front desk, room service, restaurant, bar, pet-friendly (VI), free parking (Yaba), air-conditioning, check-in and check-out times.
6. **Photos:** upload 30+ good photos per hotel (exterior with signage, reception, every room type, bathrooms, restaurant, bar) and add 2–4 new ones every month. Profiles with more recent photos get more clicks.
7. **Posts:** publish one Google Post a week (offers, events, seasonal packages such as "Detty December").
8. **Q&A:** add and answer the common questions yourself (airport distance, parking, pets, payment methods).
9. **Free booking links:** in Google Hotel Center, connect your rates through a supported booking engine or channel manager. Your direct price then appears next to Booking.com's in Google.

## 2. Reviews: the #1 ranking factor on both Google and TripAdvisor

Both platforms rank hotels on **how many** reviews you have, **how good** they are, and **how recent** they are. A steady flow of fresh 5-star reviews beats a big burst once a year.

### Build a review habit
- **Ask every guest, every time**, at checkout and again by WhatsApp within 24 hours.
- Create short links to your review pages:
  - Google: in your Business Profile, click **Ask for reviews** to copy the link.
  - TripAdvisor: in Management Center, use **Review Express** (free) to email guests after checkout.
- Put a small **QR-code card** in every room and at reception: "Enjoyed your stay? Tell others on Google" (and a second card for TripAdvisor).
- Train front-desk staff to mention it warmly at checkout. This single habit makes the biggest difference.

### WhatsApp message template (send the day after checkout)
> Hello {first name}, thank you for staying with us at Ikad Hotel & Suites, Victoria Island! 🙏
> We hope everything was perfect. If you have a minute, we'd be grateful if you shared your experience here: {Google review link}
> Your feedback helps other travellers find us. Safe travels, and we look forward to welcoming you back!
> — The Ikad team

### Reply to every review
- Reply to **all** reviews within 48 hours, good and bad.
- For positive reviews, thank the guest by name and mention something specific.
- For negative reviews, apologise, don't argue, say what you've fixed, and invite them to contact the manager directly. Future guests read these replies closely.

**Target:** 10+ new Google reviews and 5+ new TripAdvisor reviews per hotel per month, averaging 4.5★ or higher.

## 3. TripAdvisor (weeks 1–4)

TripAdvisor's "Popularity Ranking" for Victoria Island hotels is based on the **quality, recency and quantity** of reviews. Your website has little effect on it.

1. **Claim both listings** at [tripadvisor.com/Owners](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Owners) and get Management Center access.
2. Make the name, address and phone match the website exactly. Add the website link.
3. Upload 30+ professional photos per hotel and write a full description with amenities and room types.
4. Turn on **Review Express** and send it to every checked-out guest who has an email address.
5. Reply to every review from Management Center.
6. Add the TripAdvisor "Reviews" widget or badge to the website **once you have 20+ reviews**. Send me the widget code and I'll add it.

## 4. Consistent listings everywhere (weeks 2–6)

Google cross-checks your name, address and phone across the web. Every listing should match the website footer exactly.

- ✅ Booking.com, Expedia, Trip.com: update name, address, phone and photos. The Yaba property also needs its own OTA listings if it doesn't have them yet.
- ✅ Hotels.ng and other Nigerian hotel and travel sites
- ✅ Nigerian business directories (e.g. VConnect, BusinessList Nigeria)
- ✅ Apple Maps (via Apple Business Connect) and Bing Places. Both are free and often forgotten.
- ✅ Facebook and Instagram pages: same name, address and phone, and a link to the right hotel page.

## 5. Website follow-ups (monthly)

- **Search Console:** submit `https://ikadhotels.com/sitemap.xml`, then check monthly for errors and see which searches bring visitors.
- **Rich Results Test:** confirm the hotel, FAQ and breadcrumb data shows as valid for `/`, `/victoria-island` and `/victoria-island/things-to-do`.
- **Fresh content:** add one helpful local article a month. Each should answer a real traveller question, for example:
  - "Hotels near Eko Hotel & Suites for event guests"
  - "Getting from Lagos airport to Victoria Island"
  - "Where to celebrate Detty December in Victoria Island"
  - "A business traveller's guide to Victoria Island"
- **Links from others:** partner with nearby event venues, corporate offices, embassies' visitor lists, wedding planners and Lagos travel bloggers. Offer corporate rates and ask to be listed as a recommended hotel with a link to your site.
- **Photos:** a professional shoot of every room type in daylight will lift clicks on Google, OTAs and the website.

## ⚠️ Never do these, because they get hotels penalised or delisted

- Buying reviews, or writing reviews for yourself, staff, friends or competitors.
- Offering discounts, gifts or refunds **in exchange for** a review. Both Google and TripAdvisor ban incentivised reviews.
- "Review gating": only asking happy guests, or screening guests before sending the review link. Ask everyone.
- Keyword-stuffing the business name, or creating fake or duplicate profiles.
- Putting star ratings on the website that aren't backed by real, visible reviews.

**Honest expectation:** nobody can guarantee a #1 position. But hotels that follow this plan consistently usually see clear movement in the Maps and TripAdvisor rankings within 3–6 months, with the biggest gains coming from steady reviews and an active Google Business Profile.
